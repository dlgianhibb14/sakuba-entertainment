SAKUBA ENTERTAINMENT WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html — the complete website.

To customize:
1. Open index.html.
2. Change FEEDBACK_EMAIL near the bottom to the email address where you want feedback.
3. Upload index.html to your web host.
4. Connect your domain (sakubaentertainment.com) to that host.

The game is embedded from the Scratch project:
https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/1163527152/

Note: the current feedback form uses mailto, so visitors need an email app configured.
For a true online form that stores submissions without opening an email app, connect the
form to a form backend such as Formspree, Google Forms, or your own server.
